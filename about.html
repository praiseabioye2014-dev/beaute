Our Story

Fleur de Beauté was born from a passion for beauty and a desire to create a space where every woman feels seen, valued and confident. We started with a simple belief: that beauty is not just about how you look, but how you feel.

Today, we’re proud to be a trusted beauty destination in Ilorin, offering professional hair, makeup, skincare and nail services with a personal touch.
